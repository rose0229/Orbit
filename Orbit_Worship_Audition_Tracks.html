Acoustic Guitar

Acoustic players are the rhythmic and harmonic anchor of our band. We're looking for players who lock in with the groove, make tasteful chord choices, and support the song without overplaying. Record yourself playing one song from start to finish.

What We're Listening For
  • Consistent, locked-in strumming rhythm
  • Clean chord transitions with no buzz or muted strings
  • Dynamics — soft in verses, full in choruses
  • Capo usage and alternative voicings when appropriate
  • Musical restraint — serving the song, not showcasing

Electric Guitar

Electric players at Orbit define the texture of our sound — from clean shimmer to driven grit. We want players who know when to fill and when to sit still. Record one song using your actual rig: amp, pedals, and the tone you'd actually bring to rehearsal.

What We're Listening For
  • Tone quality — clean, controlled, intentional
  • Timing and pocket — do you lock with the song?
  • Arrangement awareness — fills, swells, and space
  • Pedal board organization and sound transitions
  • Ability to play minimally when the song calls for it

Keys

Keys players at Orbit carry huge sonic real estate — from pad textures under the whole song to melodic piano moments that define a section. We're looking for players who understand arrangement, know when to hold back, and can read a room. Record one song on any keyboard rig you have.

What We're Listening For
  • Pad layering — sustain, swell, and release control
  • Piano voicings — open, spacious chords that support vocalists
  • Left-hand bass awareness when no bass is present
  • Sound selection — appropriate patch choices per section
  • Musical sensitivity — less is often more

Bass

Bass players are the rhythmic and harmonic foundation of the entire band. If you don't lock with the kick drum, nothing else works. We want players who are solid first, creative second. Record one song DI — no amp mic if you can help it.

What We're Listening For
  • Locked-in pocket with the kick drum pattern
  • Clean tone — no excess buzz, muting, or finger noise
  • Root movement and harmonic awareness
  • Restraint — does the bass serve the song?
  • Fills that resolve, not fills that wander

Drums

Drums set the entire energy of worship. We're looking for players who feel the song, not just keep time. Groove, ghost notes, dynamic sensitivity, and click-lock are all on the table. Set up a camera and play one full song to a reference track or click.

What We're Listening For
  • Click-track consistency — locked all the way through
  • Dynamic range — soft verses, full choruses, controlled transitions
  • Kick and snare tone and placement accuracy
  • Ghost note sensitivity and hi-hat color
  • Musical taste — when to build, when to strip back

How We Evaluate

Every audition is reviewed by Isaiah, Brecklyn, and Matt. We score across five dimensions and give every person written feedback regardless of the outcome.

CategoryWhat We're Measuring
Pitch & ToneAre you consistently in tune? Is your tone controlled and intentional across the full range of the song?
Timing & GrooveDo you lock with the track? Does your feel match the style of the song — not just the notes, but the pocket?
DynamicsCan you play or sing softly in a verse and fully in a chorus? Do you respond to the song's energy?
MusicalityDo you make musical decisions, or are you just playing the notes? Do you serve the song?
Team FitBased on your video and form, would you thrive in a collaborative, servant-hearted team environment?
Team Tiers

Orbit Worship runs a tiered team structure. Your audition determines where you start — and tiers are reviewed quarterly based on growth, availability, and team needs.

Tier 1 — Choir / BGV
You serve in the choir or as a background vocalist in larger ensemble settings. Strong pitch and blend, still developing song familiarity or stage presence. A great entry point and a vital part of our sound.
Tier 2 — Frontline
You serve on the front line as a featured background vocalist or supporting musician. Consistent, reliable, and able to handle microphone responsibilities with maturity.
Tier 3 — Lead Vocalist / WL
You lead songs or carry primary vocal responsibility. This tier requires a full-band Phase 2 audition and pastoral recommendation in addition to the video submission.
