Foundation

What We're Building

Worship ministry is not a platform for performance. It is a place of service.

Our goal is not simply to find talented musicians and vocalists. We are building a worship team that helps lead our church to see, trust, and treasure Jesus. That means we are looking for people who are spiritually mature, musically prepared, humble, teachable, consistent, and able to serve the room rather than perform for it.

What We're Looking For
  • A genuine love for Jesus and a growing walk with God
  • Commitment to Orbit Church — not just showing up to sing
  • Humility and teachability above all
  • Musical preparation and consistent timing/pitch
  • Ability to follow direction without defensiveness
  • Ability to serve a song — not overplay or over-sing
  • Awareness of the congregation you are leading
  • Reliability and follow-through
  • Healthy team presence — low drama, high trust
The Strongest Team Members

The strongest worship team members are not always the flashiest musicians. They are the ones who are prepared, humble, consistent, and able to make everyone around them better.

Skill matters. Preparation matters. Character matters more.

Purpose

What Auditions Are Designed to Reveal

The goal is not to audition people only on songs that make them feel something. The goal is to use songs that reveal whether they can serve the room, follow the arrangement, stay in time, blend, and carry worship without making it about themselves.

For Vocalists
  • Pitch accuracy and rhythmic stability
  • Dynamic control and range
  • Tone, blend, breath control, diction
  • Ability to follow a click and arrangement cues
  • Musical restraint — knowing when not to sing
  • Congregational sensitivity
  • Spiritual and emotional maturity without performance theatrics
For Musicians
  • Pocket, chord accuracy, and subdivision
  • Arrangement awareness and clean transitions
  • Ability to build dynamically — and simplify
  • Ability to follow a click and a band leader
  • Ability to serve the song rather than overplay
  • Awareness of the full band and the room
Process

Audition Format

The audition process includes two stages. Both stages are designed to see how you function in a real team environment — not just how you sound alone in a room.

Stage One · Video Submission

Submit a video of yourself singing or playing the assigned audition songs. A phone recording is fine — we simply need to clearly hear and see you.

  • Voice or instrument clearly audible
  • Playing or singing with the original song, a backing track, or a click
  • Room quiet enough for accurate evaluation
  • Video shows enough of you to observe your playing or singing
  • You are prepared and familiar with the arrangement
Stage Two · Live Audition

If invited forward, you will sing or play selected portions of the audition songs with members of our worship leadership team. We may ask you to:

  • Start or stop at a specific section
  • Sing melody or harmony on command
  • Adjust dynamics or simplify your part
  • Try a different key or follow new cues
  • Receive feedback and try something again
💡
This is not meant to be intimidating.
The live audition helps us see how you function in a real team environment. Coming in ready to receive direction — and respond without defensiveness — tells us more than your voice or instrument ever could.

Expectations

Preparation & What Not to Do

We are not expecting perfection. We are expecting preparation. A mistake is not automatically a problem. Being unprepared is.

✓ Preparation Means
  • You know the song structure
  • You know your part — and when to come in and drop out
  • You can follow the arrangement
  • You have practiced with the track, not just learned it in your head
  • You are not relying on the audition to learn the song
  • You are ready to receive feedback
✗ Please Avoid
  • Over-singing or overplaying
  • Changing the arrangement unnecessarily
  • Treating the audition like a performance
  • Adding excessive runs, fills, or solos
  • Ignoring dynamics or playing too loudly
  • Singing without regard for blend
  • Showing up unfamiliar with the song
  • Becoming defensive when given direction
  • Trying to manufacture a spiritual moment

Watch For

Red Flags by Role

These are not automatic disqualifiers — but they are signals to address directly before or during the Formation Season. Ignoring them causes larger problems later.

Vocalist Red Flags
  • Consistently sharp or flat
  • Cannot sing harmony without being dragged by the melody
  • Over-sings every chorus — no dynamic variation
  • Imitates the recording exactly but cannot adapt
  • Poor diction or swallowed words
  • Emotional intensity without pitch control
  • Cannot follow a click
  • Cannot take correction without embarrassment or defensiveness
  • Treats the audition like a performance
Musician Red Flags
  • Does not know the arrangement
  • Cannot play to a click
  • Overplays constantly — no dynamic sensitivity
  • Poor tone quality
  • Cannot simplify a part when asked
  • Cannot follow a band leader
  • Does not know song sections
  • Rushes transitions
  • Noodles between sections
  • Acts bored during simple parts
Worship Leader Red Flags
  • Talks too much between songs
  • Performs spirituality — manufactured moments
  • Cannot give clear band cues
  • Cannot recover gracefully from mistakes
  • Chooses keys that serve themselves, not the congregation
  • Has no awareness of the room's engagement
  • Confuses vocal ability with pastoral leadership

Next Steps

After the Audition

The worship leadership team will review and follow up with next steps. Not being added to the team immediately does not mean you are not valued — it may simply mean timing, fit, readiness, or role is not yet clear.

Joining the Worship Team
You are ready for the team and your tier has been determined. Formation Season begins.
Joining With a Development Plan
You are on the team with specific growth areas identified and an intentional plan in place.
Limited or Specific Role
Placed in a role that matches your current readiness — which may grow over time.
Continue Developing
You are encouraged to keep growing before the next audition cycle. Feedback will be given.
Redirected to Another Role
We see gifts and heart, but worship team may not be the right fit right now. We'll point you somewhere better.
🙏
Our Goal Is to Steward Both
We take seriously our responsibility to steward both the worship ministry and each person. Every decision is made with care — not speed, and not politics.
